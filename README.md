## English
- 36 gates of *Shishuo Xinyu* (*A New Account of Tales of the World*), one mirror each: a classical tale + a modern situation, three choices, no right or wrong — only a reflection of your heart.
- Six scholar archetypes: Pride (Ji Kang) / Melancholy (Ruan Ji) / Grace (Xie An) / Ease (Wang Xizhi) / Harmony (Wang Dao) / Return (Tao Qian).
