此 型 一 语

孤直不屈 · 宁可玉碎　——　嵇康

And yet: your pride holds a youth who won't bow — don't let it harden into a refusal to admit.

判 词

你是嵇康的传人：宁可玉碎，不为瓦全。你的底线比谁都硬，孤独也认了——但记住，傲骨要有铠甲，别把后背露给钟会们。

You are Ji Kang's heir: jade over tile. Your spine is the hardest in the room, and you pay for it in solitude — but armor your pride, and never turn your back on the Zhong Huis.

另有一面：你的傲里藏着不肯低头的少年气，别让它变成不肯认错的固执。

And yet: your pride holds a youth who won't bow — don't let it harden into a refusal to admit.
