澄怀六型之一　·　圆融周旋 · 镇之以静　·　在七十二则中出现 44 次

此 型 一 语

圆融周旋 · 镇之以静　——　王导

And yet: your harmony hides a deep wisdom — at the key moment, one word outweighs ten thousand.

判 词

你是王导的传人：圆融周旋，镇之以静。你让所有人都觉得舒服，这是本事也是负担——别让'谁都不得罪'变成'谁都不认识你'。

You are Wang Dao's heir: round, steady, disarming. Making everyone comfortable is a gift and a load — don't let 'offending no one' become 'known to no one'.

另有一面：你的和里藏着大智慧，关键处一句顶一万句。

And yet: your harmony hides a deep wisdom — at the key moment, one word outweighs ten thousand.
