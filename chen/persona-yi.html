此 型 一 语

风流自赏 · 真率任情　——　王羲之

And yet: your ease holds a spark no one can copy — guard it from the grind.

判 词

你是王羲之的传人：风流自赏，真率任情。你懂得把日子过成画面——但记得，逸不是散漫，是把功夫下在喜欢的事上。

You are Wang Xizhi's heir: free-spirited, genuine, alive. You know how to turn days into frames — but ease is not idleness; it's craft spent on what you love.

另有一面：你的逸里有旁人学不来的灵气，守护它，别被俗务磨平。

And yet: your ease holds a spark no one can copy — guard it from the grind.
