此 型 一 语

清醒的痛苦 · 以醉避世　——　阮籍

And yet: your melancholy holds the deepest tenderness — don't keep it only for your own.

判 词

你是阮籍的传人：看透世事，却把自己醉在中间。你的清醒是天赋，也是负担——别让内耗酿成主菜，把看透变成看开。

You are Ruan Ji's heir: you see through the world and drink yourself into the middle. Your clarity is a gift and a weight — don't let attrition become the main dish; turn seeing-through into letting-go.

另有一面：你的郁里有最深的温柔，别让温柔只对自己人开放。
